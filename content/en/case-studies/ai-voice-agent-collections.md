  - title: Confirm and log
    text: The CRM is updated automatically, followed by a WhatsApp, SMS or email confirmation to the customer.
  - title: Escalation
    text: Complex cases are handed to the team; every call is recorded and auditable, and outcomes feed back into the data layer.
