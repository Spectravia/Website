highlight:
  value: "97%"
  label: satisfaction rate across the programme
challenge:
  - More than 300 branches nationwide to upskill
  - AI about to be integrated into the bank
  - Staff needed to be ready both personally and professionally
  - Governance risk in retail and corporate banking
approach:
  - title: AI for everyday work
    text: A practical track for 1,000+ staff across branches and head office.
  - title: AI for managers and decision-makers
    text: A leadership track for 200 managers and decision-makers.
  - title: Shared curriculum
    text: AI governance in retail and corporate banking, prompt engineering, banking use cases, AI tools with their strengths and fallbacks, and an individual AI project for every participant.

  - value: "1,000+"
    label: staff trained in AI for everyday work

    label: would recommend the programme to others
  - value: "96%"
    label: asked for the next-level AI course
insight: Beyond satisfaction, the programme defines how value is measured — adoption, time saved, governance compliance and one live AI project per participant — with targets to be baselined together with the bank.
