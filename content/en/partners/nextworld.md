Next World delivers **subscription-based virtual reality training for workplace safety and soft skills**. Born inside a traditional safety-training institution, it combines practical training expertise with immersive technology — and has a strong presence across the Gulf region.

Its offer is deliberately simple: an **on-demand VR course library**, a **VR LMS with analytics**, and **pre-configured wireless headsets** that work out of the box. Organizations can start training immediately, without building content or managing hardware themselves.

Typical users include oil & gas and energy operators, manufacturers, construction and logistics companies, automotive and heavy-equipment businesses, and vocational training providers.

Spectravia represents Next World across the Middle East and Africa, helping organizations roll out immersive HSE and soft-skills programmes at scale.
