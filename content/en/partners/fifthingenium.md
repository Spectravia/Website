Fifth Ingenium is an Italian technology company that designs **extended reality (XR) solutions combined with artificial intelligence** for manufacturing, energy, education, healthcare and cultural experiences. Its multidisciplinary team of engineers, designers and specialists works at the meeting point of XR, AI, IoT and 5G.

All of its products are built on **XRV**, Fifth Ingenium's own cross-device platform. Its guiding principle — *pick the best device for the use case, not the use case for the device* — means customers can use AR glasses, VR headsets, smartphones, tablets or PCs without being locked into a single hardware vendor.

Deployment is just as flexible: SaaS, public or private cloud, on-premises, hybrid or fully air-gapped — a strong fit for organizations in the Middle East and Africa with **data-sovereignty and in-country hosting** requirements.

Spectravia represents Fifth Ingenium across the Middle East and Africa, with a particular focus on **BraIND** for oil & gas, energy and manufacturing, and **TINALP** for universities.
