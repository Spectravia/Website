Blippar is a London-born **augmented reality company that has been creating AR experiences for global brands since 2011**. Its tools make AR accessible to everyone — from marketers and creators to developers and educators — with experiences that run directly in the web browser, so audiences don't need to download an app.

Blippar's technology powers interactive packaging, print and outdoor advertising, 3D product visualization, face-tracked try-on, and immersive learning content. Over the years it has worked with many of the world's best-known consumer brands and publishers.

Spectravia represents Blippar across the Middle East and Africa, helping brands, agencies and educational institutions bring augmented reality to their audiences.
