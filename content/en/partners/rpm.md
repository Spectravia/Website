RPM Training Solutions — **Resource Performance Management** — is a boutique performance-transformation partner based in Dubai, with **over two decades of experience across the GCC**.

RPM designs and delivers customized, multilingual in-house programmes in leadership development, sales, customer service, communication and organizational transformation, alongside one-to-one executive coaching. Its clients span banking and financial services, healthcare, real estate, retail, automotive, industrial and government organizations.

RPM's vision is to integrate **AI and immersive learning** into its programmes. Together with Spectravia, it combines proven human-led training with next-generation technology — from AI strategy for leaders to immersive soft-skills practice.
